The Ethereal Glow Routine

Discover the secret to our signature luminous finish. In this detailed tutorial, our lead artist walks you through the exact steps to achieve a flawless, breathable base that lasts all day.

Resumo do Vídeo

1
Skin Prep Begin with a clean canvas. Apply the Hydrating Essence pressing gently into the skin.
2
Luminous Base Mix 2 drops of Illuminating Serum with your foundation for an ethereal glow.
3
Conceal & Brighten Apply radiant concealer only where needed to maintain the skin's natural texture.
4
Soft Setting Use a fluffy brush to lightly dust finishing powder on the T-zone.
